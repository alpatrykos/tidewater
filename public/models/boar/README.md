# Wild boar coat

`coat.png` is the dedicated diffuse undercoat texture used by the custom WebGPU boars.
Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-24. It is an original generated material,
not a photograph taken from the reference sites or an extracted game asset. The original
1254 x 1254 RGB pixels were preserved while adding an opaque alpha channel for the engine's
RGBA PNG decoder. The runtime creates an sRGB mip chain and reuses its repeat sampler.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square game PBR base-color texture for the dense coarse fur on a realistic adult European wild boar. Create ONLY a flat, perfectly edge-to-edge orthographic macro texture swatch, representing a 35 cm square patch of natural wild boar flank coat. Thousands of fine individually visible short tapered bristles, dense shaggy coarse guard hairs above a darker fine undercoat, subtly clumped and overlapping, hairs predominantly groomed from TOP toward BOTTOM with slight natural irregular bends. Black-brown and muted warm dark grey roots, restrained taupe and grey-brown hair shafts, sparse grey-beige agouti tips. Dense coverage without bare skin. Real biological bristle hair, NOT woodgrain, NOT striped fabric, NOT straw, NOT regular repeated parallel lines. Flat even cross-polarized diffuse illumination, moderate albedo brightness suitable for physically based lighting, no baked specular glints, no directional shadows, no highlights, no gradient, no vignette. Uniform scale and texture density across entire image, seamless tiling on all edges. Highest fine material detail. No animal silhouette, no eyes, no face, no objects, no text, no frame, no logos. Square 2048x2048 preferred.

The geometry is generated by `src/world/wildlife/BoarShapes.js` (19,408 triangles), with an
8,200-strand groom generated from the skin's actual triangles (41,000 triangles). The groom
contracts from 18 to 32 metres and is culled thereafter. Skin shadows reach the middle sun
cascade; groom shadows are restricted to the near cascade. Current and previous poses and
groom detail are kept independently for motion vectors.

Validation: CPU habitat/geometry/groom tests; actual WebGPU gait, skin and groom deformation,
normal, motion-history, culling and shadow tests; browser inspection in the island scene.
An isolated 1280x800 four-boar scene with shadows measured 3.29 ms per submitted frame on the
local Mac (30-frame wall-time mean), versus 1.33 ms with the groom hidden. This measures the
boar scene, not full-game FPS or mobile performance.
